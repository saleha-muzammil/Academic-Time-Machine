## Syllabus (Lectures 6 to 15)

- Grama Ch 3: decomposition, task graphs, concurrency, critical path, mapping
- Grama Ch 4: collective communication (broadcast, all-reduce, prefix sum) on ring, mesh and hypercube
- OpenMP: work-sharing, scoping, reduction, barriers, `nowait`

## Questions

1. Algorithm design (15): mapping a sparse matrix-vector product, fetches and efficiency
2. OpenMP (15): race condition in a parallel histogram, its fixes, barriers and `nowait`
3. Broadcast (15): one-to-all broadcast across a combined ring, mesh and hypercube network
